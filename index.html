PRODUCT 01

263 Wallet

A bold, minimal wallet for onchain USDT, built to meet fiat where you are. Send wallet to wallet with a QR scan, watch live history read straight from the blockchain, and sign in with just a phone number. No seed phrases, no jargon, no middlemen.

  • Non-custodial sign-in with just a phone number
